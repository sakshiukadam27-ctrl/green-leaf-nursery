🌿 About Green Leaf Nursery

Welcome to Green Leaf Nursery! 🌱

We provide beautiful and healthy plants for homes, gardens and indoor spaces. Our goal is to bring more greenery and freshness into everyday life.

We offer indoor plants, flower plants and outdoor plants along with simple plant-care guidance.

💚 Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage people to grow plants and create a greener and healthier environment.
