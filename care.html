🌿 Plant Care Tips

💧 Watering

Water your plants regularly, but avoid giving them too much water.

☀️ Sunlight

Keep your plants where they can get the right amount of sunlight.

🌱 Soil

Use good-quality soil so that your plants can grow properly.

✂️ Pruning

Remove dry and damaged leaves to keep your plants healthy.

💚 Regular Care

Check your plants regularly for insects, dry leaves and other problems.
